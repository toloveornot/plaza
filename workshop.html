The Workshop

for those who answered yes to serving

The work

Here the building gets done. Planters stamp identical plazas — the same question, the same declaration, the same square. No improvisation, no control needed.

The pattern is the control. Like the meetings: no headquarters, but every gathering the same steps, the same readings, the same Traditions. The plaza files are the seed. Take them whole. Plant them whole.

How to plant

  1. Take the plaza files entire — question, square, declaration, commons link. Nothing added, nothing removed.
  2. Put them on any open door — any hosting, any server, any corner of the web that will have them.
  3. Keep them free. The work was never for sale and never will be.
  4. When it stands, carry the question onward — kin to kin, person to person.

The planter's standing

  • Serve, don't govern. Trusted servants keep the work; they do not rule it.
  • Identical, not original. Your plaza is not your expression — it is the pattern, faithfully kept.
  • Free, always. No price, no gate, no name on the door.
